OpenJFX 26
----------

javafx-base, javafx-graphics, and javafx-controls binary modules for the
platform named in the artifact filename.

https://github.com/openjdk/jfx/tree/26-ga

Maven coordinates:
- org.openjfx:javafx-base:26
- org.openjfx:javafx-graphics:26
- org.openjfx:javafx-controls:26
